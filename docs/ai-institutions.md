# AI institutions

Rival civilizations now plan granaries, barracks and forums through `rust/src/sim/ai_investment.rs`. TypeScript publishes observations from the existing entity/structure indices and executes normal construction, training and achievement commands. Human governors do not receive these new automatic orders.

- **Granaries:** one per city, including pending orders. The planner needs at least four completed farms and a funded treasury/income margin. It ranks a bounded neighborhood around the city's farm cluster by surviving farm coverage and shorter delivery distances, penalizing displaced farmland. The normal placement validator handles borders, occupancy, crop relocation and city ownership. Harvest bonuses and courier storage choices remain in the existing granary system.
- **Barracks:** one per city when Urban Militia is unlocked, the city has at least 24 residents, and recurring income can cover the additional 30-gold upkeep plus a margin. Completed barracks enable a native target of 30% pikemen. Gunpowder enables up to 25% musketmen when the treasury and income can sustain their additional wages. Pikemen replace the swordsman target; funded muskets replace the archer target, retaining mounted roles and a frontline. The existing allocator and school-training scheduler perform actual recruitment/retraining. Queued training wages count against the committed budget.
- **Forums:** one per realm after Cultural Identity, with at least 12 local residents, an eligible affordable achievement and the Leader near the planning city. During ordinary safe home service, the Leader can approach the forum and purchase one eligible achievement per strategy pass. Native selection follows the actual prerequisite graph, prioritizing science efficiency, recovery, culture and subsequent economic/defensive branches. The normal command checks physical reach, culture, ownership and prerequisites; owned achievements are never repurchased.

All three construction decisions preserve the existing productive-development reserve and two intervals of the new building's upkeep. Granaries, barracks and forums charge rival realms their normal upkeep (3, 30 and 3 gold per economy interval respectively). Their costs are shared native constants, also used by the ordinary construction and maintenance paths. Other rival-building upkeep rules are unchanged.

Composition is refreshed with the existing due-faction resource-planning cohort, even while a city has unfinished productive work. Barracks and forums follow the `militaryPurchases` capability; granaries remain economic development. No new per-frame world or path scans are introduced. Granary planning samples at most 256 city farms and ranks at most 256 candidate sites. Physical validations continue through the city's existing continuation queue, with at most four ordinary validations per turn and a pause after a crop-relocation preflight. Treasury/income eligibility is checked again after a suspended validation, before ordering construction.

Planner scratch buffers are transient ECS resources. Buildings, composition targets and purchased achievements use the existing save fields. This implementation provides the missing decisions; it does not establish a measured balance improvement without further headless comparisons.
