# Field cannon

Research **Field cannon** after **Gunpowder** and **Siege Engines**. It unlocks a **2×2 Foundry** costing **600 gold**, with 750 HP and a 60-second base construction time.

Open a completed friendly foundry with the Leader nearby. It shows the same faction-wide army composition sliders as the other military schools, including **Field cannon**. Raising the cannon count routes available retinue soldiers to a foundry for free retraining; lowering the count returns surplus cannon to the requested roles. New retinue recruits can also fill the requested cannon quota. No purchase or conversion fee is charged for the cannon. The foundry building retains its normal construction price.

The cannon uses the selected **1C slender barrel-and-two-wheels artwork at native 16×16 resolution**, with a rolling animation and a steady barrel while firing. Its logical footprint is one tile. The foundry uses a generated 32×32 sprite displayed across its four-tile footprint. UI icons were generated separately with imagegen. Cannon art and prompts are recorded in `public/assets/units/field-cannon-simple/source.json`.

- **Movement** Cannons move directly in any direction and take 2.5 game seconds to reach top speed from rest on a clear, straight route. They carry speed through waypoints, lose momentum on sharp turns, and brake as they reach their destination. Top speed is 75% of normal infantry base speed (2.97 tiles per second before road and formation modifiers). Dirt-road, paved-road and temporary whaling bonuses apply. Off-road travel is permitted at 20% of the cannon’s normal base speed. Shared weighted paths prefer completed roads and bridges, while respecting ordinary terrain, walls, ramps and faction access. In traffic, cannons look for a detour or back out to make room before continuing. Cannons follow the Leader in the rear of ordinary and drilled formations, can be held, and accept individual land destinations.
- **Combat** Field cannons share the fixed siege cannon’s 12-tile base range, four-second firing interval, research and equipment effects, downhill range bonus, and wall multiplier. Cannonballs deal one-eighth damage to units. Splash deals 50% of the applicable damage within two tiles, including to friendly units and buildings. Roads and bridges are immune. Cannons stop to fire. Keeps have defensive-building priority.
- **Upkeep** is 120 gold every 30 simulated seconds per cannon, or 84 with developed peat in the supplying city. The first bill is due 30 seconds after conversion. An unpaid cannon deals half damage, has half defense, and shows a flashing red warning. It regains full strength at its next paid service interval.
- **Durability:** 200 base HP and 3 base defense.

Conversion rules, shared bounded weighted route fields, movement, firing decisions and upkeep live in `rust/src/sim/field_cannons.rs` in the game's Bevy World. The TypeScript bridge publishes indexed observations and mirrors native results. Target searches and ordinary route-goal decisions run at most once a second; explicit movement commands invalidate the route immediately. Road topology has maintained reference counts and revision-based field invalidation. Resident native movement geometry supplies off-road and terrain constraints. All cannon route fields share a bounded expansion budget.

The UI and multiplayer command path both validate ownership, research, school availability and composition requirements. The foundry panel is refreshed once a second while open.

Art provenance and full generation prompts are in `scratch/2026-11-16-foundry/art-manifest.json`; the paused UI fixture and screenshot are in the same directory. Verification follows the repository restriction: compile Rust/WASM, type-check TypeScript, and run only `npm run test:startup`. No playtest or headless simulation is run.
