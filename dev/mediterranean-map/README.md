# Mediterranean geographic world

Choose **Single player → World shape → Mediterreanean**. The UI spelling matches the requested label; the code identifier is `mediterranean`. The default map is 384 × 199 tiles, covering 12°W–42°E and 27°N–49°N. The fixed geographic footprint also works with explicit development dimensions. Procedural landscape selection is disabled for this mode. Fractal remains the default world.

The game uses the existing native ground, tree, marsh, cliff and stair artwork. This is a playable geographic world with six standard civilizations and eleven connected starting towns, not a reconstruction of historical political borders or armies. The seed varies local groves, openings and starting locations; town clearings and necessary mountain passes also vary. Coastlines and river source lines remain fixed. The shared multiplayer selector also offers the mode; its server retains the existing compact 128 × 104 match dimensions.

## Landscape

- **Rivers:** 133 clipped Natural Earth river sections, including lower-ranked tributaries. Redundant 2×2 creek blocks are thinned only when all neighboring channel ports remain connected. After mouth repair, one pass removes terminal side branches only one or two tiles long, retaining at least two arms at each junction. Longer tributaries, unbranched headwaters, sea/lake outlets and map-edge connections remain. Removed cells recover their original terrain and climate before ecology and ledges are generated; downstream routing is computed afterward. Source ranks 1–3 retain broad water; smaller reaches use the existing four-native-pixel `irrigation/ditch.png` channel and matching inlet art, with four animation frames. These creek tiles retain grass or sand beneath the water and remain freshwater sources. Boats, docks and boat fishing require broader water; shallow fords retain walking crossings. Outlet-distance routing prevents cyclic `channelNext` pointers; ranks and visual widths are gameplay approximations, not measured ancient discharge. Tiny mouth gaps are connected to nearby sea/lake cells.
- **Vegetation:** REVEALS-constrained woody abundance combines with regional moisture, two scales of spatial variation, seeded local openings and greener riverbanks. Temperate, dry and conifer tree families vary with latitude, moisture and elevation. Riverine grass patches use native marsh artwork. Town footprints, cliffs, stairs and their approaches remain clear.
- **Topography:** Lightly smoothed physical elevation defines connected contours at 300, 750, 1,250, 1,800 and 2,350 metres. Each higher shelf is inset by two tiles so its native ledge has a lower landing; the default review map supports four practical levels. The Alps now form a continuous terraced range rather than isolated plateau stamps. Narrow creeks cross the terraces while major waterways cut valleys. Each walkable shelf connects through correctly oriented stairs; town clearings and narrow saddle passes preserve starting routes. Native ledge/apron palettes stay consistent, and the original sampled elevation remains unchanged. Ledge geometry is a gameplay abstraction, not a literal vertical cliff at every contour.
- **Alpine waterfalls:** After relief generation, inferred mountain tributaries descend through valid south-facing waterfall lips into existing water. Seed `MED-200BC` adds five waterfall tributaries, with 34 new channel cells. Every new route follows non-increasing physical elevation and terrace levels; its native banked channel matches the waterfall artwork. Level fords preserve access where needed, and routes that isolate walkable ground are rejected. Existing elevations, ledge tiers, stairs, mapped river routing and narrow-creek artwork remain unchanged. These local streams are gameplay inferences, not identified historical waterfalls; other facing directions await suitable native artwork.
- **Straits:** Gibraltar, Dardanelles and Bosphorus use explicit one-cell sea corridors because their physical widths are near or below the game grid spacing.

## Sources and historical limits

The checked-in [runtime data](../../src/world/data/mediterranean-data.ts) is generated by [build-mediterranean-data.py](../../scripts/build-mediterranean-data.py). [sources.json](sources.json) records exact download URLs and SHA-256 hashes for all six input files.

**Historical vegetation:** Fyfe, R. M., Githumbi, E., Trondmann, A.-K., Mazier, F., Nielsen, A. B., Poska, A., Sugita, S., Woodbridge, J., LandClimII contributors and Gaillard, M.-J. (2021), *A full Holocene record of transient gridded vegetation cover in Europe*. PANGAEA, [doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.937075](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.937075), CC BY 4.0. Uses June 2021 TW6, 2200–1700 cal BP, approximately 250 BC–AD 250; this contains 200 BC but is not an exact-year dataset. Methods: Githumbi et al. (2022), [doi:10.5194/essd-14-1581-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-1581-2022).

Raw CSV values are percentages despite the landing-page wording. The builder divides them by 100, rejects missing-coordinate/no-data rows, and joins the quality workbook's `1700-2200` column. `ET + ST` includes woody shrubs as well as trees. Cereals are not a comprehensive cultivated-land reconstruction. The cropped region has 134 supported 1° cells (80 higher-quality, 54 lower-quality), covering about 13.5% of the land. All placement at game-tile scale is inferred, including within supported cells.

**Relief:** GMT's `earth_relief_06m_p.grd`, SRTM15+ V2.7 derivative, 6 arc-minute sampling and approximately 32 km Gaussian filtering. [GMT documentation](https://www.generic-mapping-tools.org/remote-datasets/earth-relief.html); Tozer et al. (2019), [doi:10.1029/2019EA000658](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019EA000658). The original V2 grids have a [CC BY 4.0 release](https://figshare.com/articles/online_resource/Tozer_et_al_2019_SRTM15_GMT_Grids/7979780); the GMT derivative has no separately asserted license identifier here. Modern elevation is an ancient-terrain proxy. Smoothing can mix negative bathymetry into small coastal islands, so native land placement uses Natural Earth geometry rather than an elevation threshold.

**Coastline, lakes and rivers:** Natural Earth v5.1.2, pinned commit `f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776`; [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). `10m` denotes 1:10 million map scale, not 10-metre precision. Lake features marked as reservoirs are excluded. Modern rivers, deltas and shorelines still need local historical correction where exact ancient geography matters.

Gaps use explicit ecological priors: temperate northern woodland, Mediterranean woodland/scrub mosaics, dry southern/eastern interiors, Atlas and coastal uplands, and greener low river corridors. Pollen residuals use the nearest twelve sites, a 140 km Gaussian scale and 350 km cutoff; source quality weights are 1 and 0.45. Influence fades with distance, then aridity and approximate treeline limits apply. These are authored assumptions, not fitted confidence intervals. Background rationale includes Woodbridge et al. (2018), [doi:10.1111/jbi.13379](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.13379); Kröpelin et al. (2008), [doi:10.1126/science.1154913](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1154913); Bernhardt et al. (2012), [doi:10.1130/G33012.1](https://doi.org/10.1130/G33012.1). Their numerical datasets are not separate inputs.

## Rebuild and verify

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m venv scratch/mediterranean-data-env
scratch/mediterranean-data-env/bin/pip install -r dev/mediterranean-map/requirements.txt
scratch/mediterranean-data-env/bin/python scripts/build-mediterranean-data.py --download
npx vitest run tests/mediterranean-world.test.ts
npm run build
```

The builder reuses the original downloaded inputs under `scratch/2026-11-04-mediterranean-200bc/source/`; alternatively pass `--source-dir PATH`. `--download` obtains missing files and verifies pinned hashes. No network or Python dependency is used at game runtime. Elevations are quantized to 25 metres; woody/moisture fields use 16 levels. Source-scale evidence GeoJSON goes to `scratch/2026-11-04-mediterranean-integration/data/`.

With the Vite dev server running, `node scripts/check-mediterranean.mjs` verifies the actual menu, generated game, simulation and desktop/mobile rendering. Images and reports go to `scratch/2026-11-04-mediterranean-mountains-creeks/`. Its `creek-assets.html` compares the existing water assets. Unit tests check a substantial three-tier Alpine range, access to every walkable raised floor, connected starts, native stairs, clear ledges, vegetation variation, deterministic generation and preserved ford metadata. `tests/creeks.test.ts` verifies source pixels, animation, water connections, freshwater uses and boat restrictions.


## Full-map file

`node scripts/export-mediterranean-map.mjs` exports seed `MED-200BC` using the actual terrain, ford and vegetation renderers. Pass a seed as the first argument to export another starting layout. It writes `output/mediterranean-full-map.html`, a self-contained offline viewer with embedded Pixel Operator font and map images, plus `output/mediterranean-full-map.png` and `output/mediterranean-ground.png`. Both PNGs are 6,144 × 3,184 pixels at the original 16px sprite resolution. The viewer opens with the entire basin visible and supports pan, zoom, region shortcuts, a vegetation toggle, optional starting-town markers and PNG downloads. It is a static generated landscape snapshot; the markers show gameplay starting locations. Sources and historical limits travel inside the file.

`node scripts/check-mediterranean-map-export.mjs` checks offline loading, fit, pan/zoom, layers, downloads and mobile layout. Reports and screenshots go to `scratch/2026-11-04-mediterranean-full-map/`. `tests/river-spurs.test.ts` verifies short-branch pruning, preserved headwaters/outlets, exact ground restoration and valid acyclic downstream links.

## Animated Alps file

`node scripts/export-mediterranean-map.mjs --alps` exports the Alpine section of the same generated world to `output/mediterranean-alps.html`, `output/mediterranean-alps.png` and `output/mediterranean-alps-ground.png`. The crop is 87 × 41 tiles, or 1,392 × 656 native pixels. The self-contained viewer embeds all four original waterfall frames at 10 fps, with pause, location markers and a **Next waterfall** button, alongside the standard pan, zoom, vegetation and PNG controls. A custom seed can be supplied alongside `--alps`.

`node scripts/check-alps-waterfalls.mjs` checks the exported animation, waterfall navigation, offline loading, PNG download and mobile layout. Reports and screenshots go to `scratch/2026-11-05-alps-waterfalls/`. `tests/mediterranean-waterfalls.test.ts` verifies downhill tributary routes, valid native waterfall lips, unchanged terrain and mapped rivers, preserved distant vegetation and the absence of short terminal spurs.
