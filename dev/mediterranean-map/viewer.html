About this map

A playable Mediterranean landscape approximation around 200 BC, not an exact-year reconstruction. Local vegetation is inferred from regional evidence; elevation, shorelines and rivers use modern geographic proxies.

  • Vegetation: Fyfe et al. (2021), REVEALS / LandClimII, PANGAEA dataset, CC BY 4.0. TW6 covers 2200–1700 cal BP, approximately 250 BC–AD 250. Methods: Githumbi et al. (2022).
  • Elevation: GMT earth relief, derived from SRTM15+ V2.7. Tozer et al. (2019); original source grids released under CC BY 4.0.
  • Coastlines and waterways: Natural Earth v5.1.2, public domain.
  • Game artwork: Shade’s Puny World (CC0) and Puny World Medieval (purchased project license), including the existing slim water artwork.
