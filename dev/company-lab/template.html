Controls & measurements

Control: click a soldier or company card; Shift adds to the selection. Drag a box to select several companies. Right-click to give the selected order. Double-click a card to center the camera. Select either army to control both sides.

Camera: wheel to zoom, middle-drag to pan, or Fit map to reset. Space pauses; 1 / 2 / 4 change speed. Escape clears an unfinished selection.

Measurements: frame CPU includes simulation and drawing, not browser presentation. Path time is cumulative navigation CPU since restart. Long waits counts soldiers awaiting movement for over four seconds, including queues at narrow passages. Soldiers already fighting or at their destination are excluded. Idle drift measures settled-soldier movement in tiles per second; zero means no milling. Cohesion is the 95th percentile distance from each soldier to its company.
