Reading the forest measurements

Wooded land counts forest and jungle tiles as a share of all dry land. Unwooded land is the remaining dry land, including hills, beaches and other terrain. A solid 2 × 2 patch is four neighboring tiles all occupied by trees.

Open a woodland detail to inspect the mixture of trees and clearings. The inspector also measures how many tree tiles touch reachable, unwooded land to their north, south, east or west. Water and cliffs do not count as clearings. Woodland ↻ cycles through dense regions, making it easy to judge the busiest forests.

Each row shares a seed; each column uses a different world shape. All nine worlds use the same current game settings. The overview uses terrain colors; zoom in to see the original tile and tree artwork. These are frozen snapshots of generated worlds, with all data and art embedded for offline review.
