How to read this comparison

Families guide the landscape's structure. Start with the full maps, then open Landform detail for an actual river, relief body, lake or coastal location. Use the feature buttons to visit other measured locations. Native 1× / 2× switches to exact 16 or 32 pixels per tile.

Terrain uses overview colors at wide zoom and the existing tile artwork up close. Height shows the continuous elevation field on a fixed 0–1 scale; it is different from the game's discrete raised floors. Provinces colors each tile by its assigned landform; dashed ellipses in the inspector show the regional guides, not hard boundaries. These diagnostic views do not add new artwork to the game.

Raised land is the share of dry tiles assigned a raised floor. Flat interiors have a walkable 3 × 3 neighborhood at the same floor level. Low corridors are unraised land between raised ground to the east and west or north and south, within six tiles without crossing water; this is a geometric proxy, not a geological valley classification.

Relief shapes are cardinally connected raised bodies with at least nine tiles. Elongation is measured from their principal axes: below 1.8 is compact, at least 3 is long; orientation counts include bodies with elongation of at least 1.5. Woodland checks count a reachable cardinal opening next to each tree and fully wooded 2 × 2 squares. All land shares use dry land as the denominator.

These are complete, frozen world snapshots. Generation time measures the generator call on the build machine, excluding compression and review measurements. Maps, artwork and controls are embedded in this file and work offline.
