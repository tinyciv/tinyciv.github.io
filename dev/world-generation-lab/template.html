Landscape design & research

Ridges and woodland share broad regional patterns, with smaller groves and outcrops nested inside. Wetter terrain favors forest; usable upper floors can carry trees too. Cliff faces, ramps, passes, and palace cores stay clear.

The design draws on research into valley spacing and forest patch sizes. The exact tile sizes, woodland coverage, and pass spacing are gameplay choices, not a calibrated erosion model.

Worlds and mountain ranges include native woodland. Switch it off to inspect cliffs and routes.
