How this camp adapts

    Outer wallMiddle wallInner wall

    Fit the camp in five passes

    1. 01Read the ground

      Separate dry building ground, reachable approaches, water, and cliff edges.

    2. 02Choose a connected site

      Search nearby clearings for enough usable space and a route to the outside.

    3. 03Fit the defensive wards

      Adjust the enclosure to the site while keeping walls one tile thick and lanes between tiers.

    4. 04Connect gates and tents

      Reserve the approach and internal roads, then fit tents with reachable entrances.

    5. 05Accept or try again

      Check every placement and route. A site that cannot support a valid camp is rejected.

    Fixed rules: native sprite scale · single-tile walls · no overlapping structures · open tent entrances · a connected route through the gates

    Working placement study · Only tents, wooden walls, guard towers, signs, and roads are added to the landscape. All artwork is embedded for offline review.
