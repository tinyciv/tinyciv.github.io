These are unmodified original PNG sheets, renamed for local URLs:
  Mountains.png -> mountains.png
  Grass/Hills.png -> hills-grass.png
  Desert/Hills Desert.png -> hills-desert.png

BTV crops individual sprites and displays them with nearest-neighbor scaling.
Only the two broad, rounded mountain variants (columns 0 and 2) are used;
mountains are displayed wider and lower than the original sprite proportions.
